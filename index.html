Work With Us

Bread of Life Homeschool Community

Develop effective Structured Literacy practices and build organizational capacity that improves student outcomes.

What we do

Transform the Mind, Body, and Soul

True education is the harmonious development of the physical, mental, and spiritual powers.

We provide the resources and community to help families reclaim their God-given role as the primary educators, moving away from the secular, competitive factory model of common schools.

For Parents (Priests & Teachers)

We empower you to cultivate a principle-based home life, shielding your child’s mind and establishing lessons of respect and self-control before formal academics begin.

For Students (Individuality)

We focus on character over competition. Our curriculum nurturs your child’s unique talents at their own pace, ensuring they are not just "mere reflectors of other men's thoughts."

For the Home (Physical Vigor)

Coaches will build their practice to deliver student-focused, dialogical coaching that helps teachers dive through data, analyze current practices, explore instructional strategies, and target instruction to meet their students’ diverse learning needs.

Learn More

Our Method

Applying The Blueprint of True Education which create approach to ensures that the work of education and redemption are one. Our curriculum unfolds through three key foundational pillars.

Phase 1

The First School

We establish the home as the ultimate foundation. We guide parents in building a curriculum where the table is the desk, prioritizing the moral and spiritual atmosphere of the home above all else.

Learn how

Phase 2

Nature's Lesson Book

We move learning beyond the four walls. By integrating outdoor learning and our agricultural modules, children learn the dignity of manual labor and observe God’s handiwork in the natural world.

Learn how

Phase 3

The Work of Redemption

Our ultimate goal is to restore the Image of God in the student. Every subject is anchored in the "Rock of Truth," providing the moral compass necessary to navigate life's challenges.
